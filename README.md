# NARZY
### AI-Powered Financial Risk and Fraud Analytics

NARZY is an AI-powered early warning system designed to identify listed companies that may be at risk of financial distress or potential accounting manipulation.

## Problem Statement
Financial distress and suspicious financial patterns may go unnoticed until serious losses occur. Analysts also have limited time to review large numbers of companies.

## Our Solution
NARZY aims to:
- Identify companies showing signs of financial distress.
- Analyse financial statements and important financial ratios.
- Screen for potential accounting irregularities.
- Explain the main factors behind each alert.
- Prioritise companies for analyst review.

## Planned Features
- Bankruptcy risk prediction
- Altman Z-Score analysis
- Beneish M-Score analysis
- Piotroski F-Score analysis
- Financial anomaly detection
- Explainable risk alerts

## Technology Stack
- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas and NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- SHAP

## Project Status
Under development. Features and results will be updated as implementation progresses.

## Disclaimer
NARZY is a financial risk screening prototype. An alert indicates a need for further investigation and does not prove fraud or predict a certain default.
